About the college

Emmanuel Baptist College (EBCO) is a Christian secondary school in Tanke, Ilorin, built on discipline and academic rigour. Our aim is to bring up your child in a Godly way and prepare them for the world beyond school.

Our motto, God With Us, is on the crest: a cross standing on the rock of faith. We believe every child has talents, strengths and leadership capacity, and our teachers work to bring them out.

With dedicated teachers, well-equipped facilities and a firm stance on discipline, we guide students to reach their highest potential.

Our core values

Academic excellence

A standard of intellectual rigour and steady curiosity that drives academic distinction.

Godliness

Strong Christian ethics, honesty, integrity and faith in everything we do.

Discipline

Self-regulation, moral uprightness, and respect for authority and for one another.

Innovation

Critical thinking, creative coding and modern solutions that ready students for the future.

Milestones of success

  1. 2000

    College inception

    EBCO opens on 9 October 2000 with a small group of passionate teachers and eager students, combining Christian character with secondary education.

  2. 2005

    National WAEC awards

    Recognised among the top schools in Kwara State for 100% distinction rates in WAEC and NECO examinations.

  3. 2015

    ICT and e-learning

    Computer resource centres and science laboratories bring coding and digital literacy into the main curriculum.

  4. 2026

    Modern campus upgrades

    Campus expansion, boarding upgrades, hybrid digital classrooms and a new student information portal.
